- Users should be able to register an account to protect their sim. On their unique URI, they can add a username and password to correlate that sim with their username. once a sim is protected, the backend will check for authentication before serving the content of the sim. If the authentication fails the user is redirected to the home page. At the home page a new user has the option to create an account which will also create their default sim, or just create a sim with a unique URL.
- The read-only share view should be the same as the regular view, but the import, share buttons should be removed, the "Bookmark your secret link!" warning shouldn't show. And Edit buttons should instead say "show details" and all text fields on the page should be disabled. Finally, I like the "Create your own" button, but it should instead say "Copy Simulation to Edit"
